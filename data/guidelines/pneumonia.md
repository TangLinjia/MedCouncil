# Community-Acquired Pneumonia

## Diagnosis

Community-acquired pneumonia may present with fever, cough,
dyspnea, sputum production, and chest discomfort.

Chest imaging may be used when pneumonia is suspected.

## Treatment

Treatment depends on disease severity, comorbidities,
drug allergies, recent antibiotic exposure, and local
antimicrobial resistance patterns.

## Follow-up

Patients should seek urgent medical evaluation if they develop
worsening respiratory distress, confusion, hypotension,
persistent hypoxemia, or rapidly worsening symptoms.
